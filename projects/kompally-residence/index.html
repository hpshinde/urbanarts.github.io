Kompally Residence

At Kompally, the kitchen is not backstage. It sits openly within family life, its planning, cabinetry and lighting developed as carefully as the rooms and garden around it.

Status
Completed
Urban Arts role
Design + build
Scope
Interior architecture · Kitchen design · Furniture · Landscape · Turnkey implementation
Recognition
Häfele Kitchen Ideas Design Challenge 2021 · Winner, South Zone · Runner-up, All India · Open Kitchen, Built Category

Project note

Urban Arts carried the interior from space planning to making and installation. The open kitchen—winner, South Zone and runner-up, All India in the Häfele Kitchen Ideas Design Challenge—was conceived as a social room adjoining the dining and family spaces. Custom joinery, furniture and wall treatments continue that close attention to use through the bedrooms, while the landscape extends daily life into shaded garden rooms.
