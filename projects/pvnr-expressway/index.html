Urban infrastructure · Hyderabad

PVNR Elevated Expressway

Architectural design visualisation for the piers and undercarriage of Hyderabad’s elevated expressway—the parts of this large infrastructure most directly experienced from the street.

Status
Completed
Scope
Architectural design visualisation · Piers and undercarriage

Project note

Urban Arts was not responsible for the engineering of the expressway. Its commission addressed the architectural articulation and visualisation of the pier forms and undercarriage, testing how repetition, span and the view from below could give civic character to an otherwise purely infrastructural system.
