Museum restoration and extension · Hyderabad

AP State Archaeology Museum

Restoration of the existing museum together with the architectural and interior design of its extension, bringing old fabric, new galleries and exhibition requirements into one project.

Status
Completed
Scope
Restoration · Extension architecture · Interior and exhibition design

Project note

The commission crossed the usual boundary between conservation and new work. Urban Arts addressed the restoration of the existing museum, designed the new extension and developed its interiors and exhibition environment. Display cases, lighting, circulation and architectural detail were coordinated so that the collection—not the apparatus around it—remains visually primary.
