Residential interiors · Hyderabad

Ravi Residence

Living, dining and circulation are treated as one continuous interior, while cabinetry, screens and lighting give individual rooms their own degree of enclosure and character.

Status
Completed
Urban Arts role
Design + build
Scope
Interior architecture · Custom cabinetry · Lighting · Turnkey implementation

Project note

Rather than divide the shared spaces with walls, the design uses furniture, ceiling planes, lighting and changes in material to establish different territories. Purpose-built storage is absorbed into the architecture, leaving the main sequence open while allowing each bedroom to respond to a different occupant.
